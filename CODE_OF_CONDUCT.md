# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation
in the JEETrack community a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

JEETrack's community includes JEE aspirants who are often teenagers under
real academic pressure — a respectful, encouraging tone matters more here
than in a typical open-source project.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the students using JEETrack
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior in issues/discussions
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional or academic setting

## Enforcement responsibilities

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying standards and will take
appropriate, fair corrective action in response to any behavior deemed
inappropriate, threatening, or harmful — including removing comments,
closing issues/PRs, or banning contributors for behavior they deem
inappropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces (issues,
discussions, pull requests) and when an individual is officially
representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the maintainer via the contact listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
